The Chandra Promise

Silver as Pure
as Moonlight.

Every Chandra piece is crafted from Pure 925 Sterling Silver — BIS Hallmarked and fully traceable. We work with master artisans across India's most storied silversmithing traditions, combining centuries of craft knowledge with designs made for the world today.

From the delicate filigree of Odisha to the bold Thappa work of Rajasthan, each technique we employ carries the weight of generations and the lightness of something made with love.

India's Ancient Craft

Made by Hand.
Made in India.

Each Chandra piece carries the fingerprint of its maker. Master artisans trained in techniques passed down through generations. We preserve a living heritage while crafting jewellery for modern lives.

05

Ancient Techniques.
One Silver.

  1. 01
    Thappa

    Die-stamped silver sheets pressed into intricate repeated patterns with precision tools passed through generations

  2. 02
    Chitaai

    Fine line engraving etched directly onto polished silver surfaces by master artisans who trained for years

  3. 03
    Rawa

    Granulation — tiny silver granules fused to the surface through controlled heat, creating luminous textured brilliance

  4. 04
    Filigree

    Delicate twisted wire work forming intricate lace-like motifs, a living tradition of Indian silversmithing craft

  5. 05
    Marcasite

    Iron pyrite gems hand-set in sterling silver for timeless antique radiance and depth
